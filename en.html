Quant Terminal

US & Korean stock recommendation system stock-recommendation-systemCode and docs on GitHub ↗
  1. Public dataYahoo·SEC·FRED·KRX·DART
  2. Normalizesource & status per value
  3. Gatesdata · bear market · sector
  4. Frozen-model scoringfactor z-scores
  5. Top 5reasons to pick or drop
  6. Hash-chained ledgertracks picks afterward
  7. Web terminalTelegram alerts
Why
I needed a tool that explains "why this stock" with evidence, without paid data, and lets me check later whether each pick was actually right. I left out auto-trading on purpose. The system only provides the evidence and the record, and a person checks and decides every buy and sell. This human-in-the-loop design keeps a data error or a model mistake from turning straight into an order.
Built
  • Data collection & normalization — collects Yahoo, SEC EDGAR, FRED, KRX, OpenDART and ECOS, tags every value with its source and status, and never fills gaps with zero
  • Recommendation model — momentum, quality, value and low-volatility factor z-scores, a bear-market filter, sector caps and ATR-based position sizing produce a Top 5
  • Investment committee — five rule engines in the style of Buffett, Munger, Graham, Lynch and Druckenmiller judge each stock separately; the committee decides by rule, not by averaging, and builds six simulated portfolios (kept apart from the score, no orders)
  • Verification — pre-registration, automatic look-ahead leak checks, and 5-year backtests against a challenger model and an SPY baseline
  • Operations — local web terminal (about 89 APIs), daily launchd automation, atomic writes via fsync and rename
Gained
2,065tests passing
80.0–80.7%actual coverage of the 80% price range
96% / 90%earnings-date estimates (KR/US)
Lost
It has not yet shown that its ranking predicts returns. The Korean model is still experimental, and free data meant ever more handling for delays and gaps.
Learned
"Recommending" and "predicting returns" are different claims. Recording results in advance, where they can't be changed, is what keeps me from fooling myself later.

Horizon

Binance spot & futures trading bot + coin direction predictor horizon_bot_betaCode and docs on GitHub ↗
  1. Binanceccxt prices & orders

Why
My earlier Bithumb bot had no tests and no paper trading, so I could not prove its strategy worked. I built a new bot that paper-trades by default and is verified against criteria set in advance.
Built
  • Regime-based strategy — ADX and ATR split the market into trending, ranging and low-volatility regimes, each with its own entry rules and a 0–4 conviction score
  • Risk management — 0.75% risk per trade and a 5% total cap, scaling down at 5% drawdown and stopping at 10%, an order-book slippage limit
  • Order consistency — event-sourced state, 15-second exchange reconciliation, stop-loss recovery after restarts, idempotent take-profits
  • Prediction & portfolio — ridge regression in numpy (walk-forward) and a cost-aware closed-form portfolio optimizer
Gained
57.5%8-hour hit rate, top 10% conviction
38,407 cases+0.227% net per case
313pytest tests
Lost
The hit rate held up, but after costs no profit was left. The v12–v18 trading tests all failed their gates, so the predictor only records and is not connected to trading.
Learned
Hit rate is not profit. Costs and turnover belong in the criteria, and rejected results have to be kept as they are for the next test to be honest.
